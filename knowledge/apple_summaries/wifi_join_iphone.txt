To join a Wi-Fi network on iPhone, open Settings, Wi-Fi and turn Wi-Fi on. Choose a network that you recognize, then enter its password if prompted. Ask the network owner or administrator for the password when you do not know it. A blue checkmark beside the network name and a Wi-Fi icon indicate that the iPhone has joined. Some public networks also ask you to accept terms before connection. Do not tell a user to enter a password into an unfamiliar webpage or to join an unknown network merely because it appears in the list. Joining a network and having working internet access are separate checks.
