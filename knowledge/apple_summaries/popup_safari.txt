Safari pop-ups on iPhone can be ordinary ads or deceptive warnings. A warning, prize, or technical-support phone number displayed by a webpage is not proof that the phone is infected. If a pop-up is unfamiliar, avoid its links and buttons; even a close button may be fake. Close the Safari tab or window instead. To review Safari protection settings, open Settings, Apps, Safari, then check Block Pop-ups and Fraudulent Website Warning. Menu names may differ with the installed iOS version. Do not infer from pasted alert text alone that a particular site or alert is trustworthy.
