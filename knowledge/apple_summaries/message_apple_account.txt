An email, text, or call claiming to be from Apple may impersonate Apple Support. A screenshot cannot verify who sent it. Do not use a link or phone number in an unexpected message to sign in or seek help. Contact Apple through a known official support route if you need to check the claim. Do not share an Apple Account password, device passcode, or verification code with a caller or website reached from the message. If you already entered your password on a suspicious page, change the Apple Account password through a trusted route and check that two-factor authentication is enabled. On iPhone, a suspicious SMS that appears to be from Apple can be reported by emailing a screenshot to reportphishing@apple.com.
